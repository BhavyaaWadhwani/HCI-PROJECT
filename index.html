Human Computer Interaction - Design Challenge

Create something to help people address a problematic behaviour

Advanced Computer Science University of Birmingham 2025-26

Overview

The Problem

University students and young professionals automatically reach for their phones during work sessions without conscious decision-making, leading to doom-scrolling episodes that disrupt focus.

Our Approach

Following the user-centric design process through 5 phases: Empathise, Define, Ideate, Prototype, and Test - backed by questionnaires (75 responses) and 15 interviews.

Proposed Solution

Focus Pao is a mobile app featuring a cute red panda mascot that helps users interrupt automatic phone-checking behaviour through gentle nudges, grayscale mode, and gamified focus sessions.

Key Features

  • 01

    Face-Down Activation

    Focus mode starts when you place your phone face down, creating a physical ritual for concentration.

  • 02

    Grayscale Fade

    Screen gradually turns grayscale to reduce visual appeal and discourage continued scrolling.

  • 03

    Mini Games

    Quick mental breaks with simple puzzles when feeling bored or exhausted.

  • 04

    Streak System

    Motivational streak tracking to build lasting focus habits through loss aversion.
